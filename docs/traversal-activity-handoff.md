## Implemented behavior/bounds

- Stats-screen deltas cover deaths, mob/player kills, MINED blocks and USED items; build/break descriptors use actual source material. Held/inventory dwell uses real stacks, independent of stack count. A consumable 64-descriptor window requests at most its eight highest-weight retained observations; weights cap at 64. No lifetime-total replay. Existing asynchronous weighted-descriptor APIs perform inference off tick; only completed futures are polled, never joined.
- At most 64 personal sessions; four inventory samples per 20 ticks, up to 41 slots each; personal request cadence >=200 ticks; four personal compositions and eight environmental jobs outstanding. Event backlog <=64. Lifecycle clearing covers dimension change, respawn, disconnect and server shutdown; late results cannot update a removed session/player component.
- Influence persistence is overworld-owned `mysticism.landmark_activity.v1`, separate from authoritative geometry/catalog persistence. <=512 overlays; normalized drift toward real event/personal-dwell vectors; individual growth contribution <=0.35, with 24000-tick half-life. Same-tick events cannot manufacture repeated growth/drift, and elapsed-time growth obeys the policy budget. Verified unions retain **summed** base/activity mass, not a maximum; summed activity may exceed 0.35 and only decays until below the individual growth ceiling. Persisted total mass is bounded at 4096: over-budget unions are rejected without truncating histories. Rendering importance separately clamps to 0..1. Source relevance separately gates at 24 blocks; importance never expands it. Nearby overlapping regions rotate deterministically across pulses.
- Source discovery retains one resolved pulse and probes **<=4 catalogue metadata records per tick**, returning at most one AABB candidate per page, then enforcing exact source dimension and strict 24-block radius. It uses exclusive stable-ID seeks, not a catalogue snapshot/list or an all-or-nothing world/result cap. Repeated pulses rotate across every eligible overlap in a stable local region; <=64 dimension/source-cell (24-block) cursor entries use LRU eviction. A pulse with no match finishes at most one circular catalogue traversal, spread over ticks. Cold metadata IO remains synchronous and record-bounded, not a wall-clock guarantee. One pending core activity transaction advances at one page/reference operation and <=256 validation leaves per tick; overlay publication follows successful guarded catalogue publication.
- Ownership uses a one-source-block-resolution sparse octree with **coarse initial leaves**: side 8 below importance 0.35, side 4 below 0.65, side 2 below 0.85, otherwise side 1. Writes refine only as importance, source bounds, or a different existing owner requires; coarse writes cannot erase another owner's fine claim. Root growth <=512 blocks, <=256 cells, <=256 update nodes, <=16 owners/landmark, <=8 claimed landmarks/player. Absolute cells/source seed IDs survive growth/reload. Unions preserve full spatial coverage and all owner histories; sorted source IDs deterministically choose overlapping cell winners. The authoritative core Ownership retains combined player claims. Failed expansion retains old immutable claims. Load validation rejects wrong list types, orphan/overlapping/unaligned/out-of-root cubes, missing/duplicate owners and excessive player quotas. This does not change extractor/terrain geometry leaf policies. No chunk IDs, source-base-vector rewriting, player teleport, or projection re-keying.
- Evolver queries actual `SpiritTerrainService.support(player)`, smooths CURRENT around the supported landmark's tangent/orbit, retains TARGET, and re-attunes toward TARGET on flight/off-support. Basis stays fixed under physical terrain. Vanilla velocity, walking, jumping, flight, sneak and drop are untouched; no forced flying. Teleport displacement >4 blocks does not inject semantic travel. <=64 spirit players receive per-tick vector work.
