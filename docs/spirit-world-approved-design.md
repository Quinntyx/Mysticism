## Projection and movement
Each player owns a high-dimensional semantic position and evolving orthonormal 3D basis. Deep flight uses ORIGINAL movement-dependent BasisIntegrator behavior: going left or right eventually turns the view so the chosen movement direction approaches the captured attuned destination, over ~1–2 seconds. No special basis-steering controls. Getting lost is intentional. Never freeze global axes or share world anchors between players.
A synced boolean cardinal component distinguishes deep/shallow. Deep is free flight. Shallow preserves the supporting Landmark and source-grid alignment; ordinary jumps briefly retain shallow. Flight toggle/double-jump or leaving the landmark enters deep automatic flight. Walking stays within the landmark without semantic region popping.
Use custom projected meshes and custom collision, NOT Minecraft block placement, ownership-restoration overlays or pretend fixed chunk worlds. Near ~5 blocks, render and collision evolve together with continuous visible holes/cliffs; no apparent floor clipping. Far surfaces may distort heavily or change with fade-in/out. Fog hides distant streaming; depth occlusion applies to glyphs/entities. Local floor continuity does not mean artificial bridges across visible cliffs.

## Source representation and travel
Entry at ANY source-world location initially looks almost identical to the source terrain/entities: postprocessing fades over that view. Entities are synced non-interactable ghosts while shallow. There are no native spirit mobs in V1. A shallow target is Landmark ID + exact source block coordinates + captured embedding vector (snapshot, NOT live following). Deep targets are concept-space locations. Invalid/changed destinations leave the player deep nearby with the landmark visible; do NOT automatically find a safe substitute landing. The external attunement can make approaching the landmark difficult; failures are intentional. Exit from valid shallow materializes at its current source coordinates. /spirit commands are initial debug controls; survival entry/attunement progression comes later.

## Landmarks and history
Landmarks are block-coordinate native THREE-DIMENSIONAL octrees, never chunk identities or permanently chunk-shaped domains. Low-resolution storage with importance/detail refinement. Accessing generated Minecraft source chunks is just an IO adapter. Exclusive authoritative ownership of covered cells; sparse/unowned regions allowed. Growth/stealing/merging requires physical 3D contiguity. Initial natural cave extraction flood-fills contiguous air, including across IO boundaries, CUT at biomes. Mountains/surface features and item palettes + biomes inform embeddings. Activity can later override biome boundaries completely when semantic regions converge.
Actual growth transfers source cells and geometry, not only ownership metadata. Dwell, held/inventory/placed/broken/used items, deaths, kills and mob spawning influence personal and nearby landmark embeddings. Importance gives semantic inertia; low-importance landmarks react more strongly to actual changes. Importance decays VERY slowly over multiple in-game years. Shopping hubs cannot dominate distant semantic neighborhoods. Read already-generated unloaded source terrain with bounded background IO; do not generate unexplored terrain for discovery.

## Selection and rendering
Radius-gated, density/cluster-aware stable representative samples with hysteresis, favoring low-distortion blends. Importance only applies inside the semantic radius. Add inverse-square proximity whose reliable importance takeover is around inner FIVE percent of radius. Handle zero distance without NaN; exact/very close targets must not remain excluded by representative importance or a stale cluster lock. Fade selected regions in/out. No global 256-record cutoff or forced catalog hydration.
Fog is true volumetric, Kuwahara optimized and running, additional saturation fade. Terrain and all visible items/avatars must use depth and the same fog. Retain dependency-free pannable/paginated tree guidebook, vanilla icons, GPU pixelated purple shimmer.

## Multiplayer and dropped items
Other players' semantic locations project through each observer basis. Avatar scale decreases with basis mismatch (normal at aligned). Deep-only touch punch/right-click requires MUTUAL normal four-block reach plus sufficient alignment and occlusion. Blend RECIPIENT basis to initiator's exact basis over ~one second; no attunement change. Further movement diverges; stationary interaction can persist. Shallow touch does nothing. NO deep combat, ever.
Dropped items are the explicit native-entity exception: use VANILLA ItemEntity for mod/magnet compatibility. Their high-dimensional trajectory falls TOWARD the item's embedding LOCATION, not along an endless directional vector. Recoverable within legitimate reach / mod magnets; ordinarily hard to recover. Despawn when no observer is in range, with normal 6000-tick maximum lifetime. No fake visual-only irreversible sacrifice. Render through observer basis with fog/depth. Observer range plus lifetime also handles drops that arrive near a concept target.
